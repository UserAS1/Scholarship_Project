How to apply on the MahaDBT

Partly read on the official portal; the rest from listings

For: Post-matric scholarships and fee schemes of several departments (social justice, OBC, tribal, minority and others).

Dates: No 2026-27 dates found on an official page. Our scheme pages note that 2026-27 applications are taken only on MahaDBT 2.0; check which address your scheme uses.

Keep ready
  • Aadhaar with a linked mobile (or biometric registration)
  • Domicile, income and caste certificates (enter the barcode if issued by Aaple Sarkar)
  • Marksheets, CAP allotment letter (professional courses), caste validity certificate
  • Aadhaar-seeded bank account
Steps
  1. Register: with Aadhaar (OTP to the linked mobile), by biometric, or without Aadhaar using email and mobile OTPs. Create a username and password.
  2. Log in as Applicant and complete your profile: personal, address, parent, qualification, hostel and current course details.
  3. Open All Schemes and apply for each scheme you qualify for.
  4. Upload papers: PDFs under 256 KB, JPEG images 5 to 20 KB.
  5. See your application ID and status under My Applied Scheme.

After you submit: Your college, then the department, approve it. If your bank account is not Aadhaar-seeded the money may not arrive; an India Post Payments Bank account is an option.

Help: Helpline: 022-49150800.
