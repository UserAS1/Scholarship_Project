How to apply on the National Scholarship Portal (Assam state schemes)

Partly read on the official portal; the rest from listings

For: Post-matric scholarships for SC, ST and OBC students of Assam, applied for on the National Scholarship Portal.

Dates: 2026-27: SC and OBC students by 30 September 2026, ST students by 6 October 2026 (Bodoland University notice).

NSP one-time registration: Required: Assam's post-matric schemes are applied for on the National Scholarship Portal itself. How to get it

Keep ready
  • OTR number
  • Caste and income certificates
  • Original bonafide certificate
  • Marksheets and fee receipt
  • Aadhaar-seeded bank account
Steps
  1. Complete your NSP OTR (see below).
  2. Log in to scholarships.gov.in with the OTR, choose the Assam state scheme for your category and fill in the application.
  3. Upload papers and submit. Print the acknowledgement.
  4. Hand the printed application and the same papers to your college (for example the DSW office) by the college's date.

After you submit: Your college verifies it on the portal (institute-level verification), then the state department.

Help: NSP helpdesk: helpdesk@nsp.gov.in.

National Scholarship Portal one-time registration (OTR)

Many states now ask for this number before you apply on their own portal.

  1. Use an Android phone. Face authentication does not work on an iPhone.
  2. Install two apps from the Google Play Store: "NSP OTR" and "AadhaarFaceRD".
  3. On scholarships.gov.in, choose One Time Registration and enter your Aadhaar number and the OTP sent to the mobile linked to your Aadhaar. You get a reference number.
  4. Open the NSP OTR app, choose "eKYC with FaceAuth", enter the reference number and OTP, and look at the camera in good light.
  5. You receive a 14-digit OTR number by SMS. Write it down: you need it for every scholarship, every year.
  • If face authentication keeps failing, check in the mAadhaar app that your biometrics are not locked. A locked biometric makes it fail.
  • The OTR is not an application. You still have to apply for each scheme.
