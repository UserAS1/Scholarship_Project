How to apply on the OASIS

Partly read on the official portal; the rest from listings

For: Post-matric scholarships for SC, ST and OBC students.

Dates: 2026-27: open when we checked; the portal gives no single last date.

NSP one-time registration: Required for ST students (stated on the portal). News reports say it is now required for all. How to get it

Keep ready
  • Caste certificate number and issue date (OBC certificates issued before 1 August 2025 must be revalidated first)
  • Income certificate
  • Aadhaar-seeded bank account, enabled for DBT
  • Last marksheet and fee receipt
Steps
  1. Complete your NSP OTR if you are an ST student (and check whether it is now asked for everyone).
  2. On OASIS choose Register, pick the district where your institution is (outside West Bengal: your home district) and enter your caste certificate number and date.
  3. Fill in the primary details, set a password, then complete the full form and upload papers.
  4. Click Verify and Lock, confirm with the OTP, and download the three-page form. It cannot be changed after locking.
  5. Hand the printed form, with your income certificate, caste certificate and bank passbook copy, to your institution.

After you submit: SC students are paid 40% first and 60% later. If your Aadhaar does not show in NPCI, contact your bank branch within 7 days. Renew in later years under Students Corner.

Help: Helpline and grievances: +91 84200 23311.

National Scholarship Portal one-time registration (OTR)

Many states now ask for this number before you apply on their own portal.

  1. Use an Android phone. Face authentication does not work on an iPhone.
  2. Install two apps from the Google Play Store: "NSP OTR" and "AadhaarFaceRD".
  3. On scholarships.gov.in, choose One Time Registration and enter your Aadhaar number and the OTP sent to the mobile linked to your Aadhaar. You get a reference number.
  4. Open the NSP OTR app, choose "eKYC with FaceAuth", enter the reference number and OTP, and look at the camera in good light.
  5. You receive a 14-digit OTR number by SMS. Write it down: you need it for every scholarship, every year.
  • If face authentication keeps failing, check in the mAadhaar app that your biometrics are not locked. A locked biometric makes it fail.
  • The OTR is not an application. You still have to apply for each scheme.
