Scholarships for students from Delhi

2 state schemes, checked against official pages. Students from Delhi can also apply for the central schemes below.

State schemes

  • Merit Scholarship for SC/ST/OBC students (college, professional and technical courses) · Delhi · ₹8,000 to ₹24,000 a year, by course group and whether you live in a hostel.
  • Post-Matric Scholarship for OBC students (PM-YASASVI) · Delhi · ₹5,000 to ₹20,000 a year, by course group.

Central schemes open to students from every state

How to apply on the e-District Delhi

From news reports and listings. The portal could not be read

For: Merit and post-matric scholarships for SC, ST, OBC and minority students run by the Department for the Welfare of SC/ST/OBC.

Dates: No 2026-27 dates found on an official page.

Keep ready
  • Aadhaar card
  • Delhi caste certificate (or Delhi domicile certificate if the caste certificate was issued elsewhere)
  • Income certificate (OBC and minority students)
  • Previous year's marksheet, bonafide certificate and fee structure
Steps
  1. On e-District Delhi choose Login/Register, then Register, with Aadhaar as the document type.
  2. Log in and pick the scholarship service for college students.
  3. Fill in the form, upload your papers and submit.
  4. Ask your college scholarship desk to verify it.

After you submit: Track the application on e-District with its application number. If you had a gap of up to three years in your studies, an affidavit has to be uploaded.
