How to apply on the Har-Chhatravratti portal

Steps read on the official portal

For: Haryana Higher Education Department scholarships, including the post-matric scholarships for SC and BC students and the state merit scholarships.

Dates: 2026-27: the portal listed open schemes until 31 December 2026 when we checked.

NSP one-time registration: Listings say post-matric SC and BC students also need an NSP OTR. The portal page does not say so. How to get it

Keep ready
  • Aadhaar card
  • Parivar Pehchan Patra (family ID)
  • Photograph and signature
  • Income, Haryana domicile and caste certificates
  • Class 10 and 12 certificates
  • Fee receipt
  • Last exam passed certificate (not for first-year students)
  • BPL certificate or father's death certificate, if they apply
Steps
  1. Click Registration if you are new; otherwise log in.
  2. Enter your Parivar Pehchan Patra (family ID), choose your name from the family list and ask for an OTP.
  3. Enter the OTP sent to the registered mobile.
  4. Check the details that fill in automatically.
  5. Choose your department and college, set a password and submit the registration.
  6. Log in with your user ID and password.
  7. Enter your course, roll number and eligibility details.
  8. Click Apply to save the application.

After you submit: The college, then the department, verify it. Track it on the portal's Track Application page; complaints go through the portal's grievance system.

National Scholarship Portal one-time registration (OTR)

Many states now ask for this number before you apply on their own portal.

  1. Use an Android phone. Face authentication does not work on an iPhone.
  2. Install two apps from the Google Play Store: "NSP OTR" and "AadhaarFaceRD".
  3. On scholarships.gov.in, choose One Time Registration and enter your Aadhaar number and the OTP sent to the mobile linked to your Aadhaar. You get a reference number.
  4. Open the NSP OTR app, choose "eKYC with FaceAuth", enter the reference number and OTP, and look at the camera in good light.
  5. You receive a 14-digit OTR number by SMS. Write it down: you need it for every scholarship, every year.
  • If face authentication keeps failing, check in the mAadhaar app that your biometrics are not locked. A locked biometric makes it fail.
  • The OTR is not an application. You still have to apply for each scheme.
