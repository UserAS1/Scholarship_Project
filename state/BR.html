NSP one-time registration: Reported as required. How to get it

Keep ready
  • Bihar residence certificate
  • Caste certificate and an income certificate issued for 2026-27
  • Bonafide certificate with fee break-up and the current fee receipt
  • Previous marksheet
  • Aadhaar-seeded bank account
Steps
  1. Complete your NSP OTR.
  2. Go to the portal for your category and choose New Student Registration; verify with OTP.
  3. Log in, fill in the form and upload papers. Pick your college code carefully: only registered institutes and courses can be chosen.
  4. Submit, print the acknowledgement and give copies of your papers to the college scholarship counter.

After you submit: The institute, then the district nodal officer, verify it. Watch for a "defective" status and fix it quickly.

National Scholarship Portal one-time registration (OTR)

Many states now ask for this number before you apply on their own portal.

  1. Use an Android phone. Face authentication does not work on an iPhone.
  2. Install two apps from the Google Play Store: "NSP OTR" and "AadhaarFaceRD".
  3. On scholarships.gov.in, choose One Time Registration and enter your Aadhaar number and the OTP sent to the mobile linked to your Aadhaar. You get a reference number.
  4. Open the NSP OTR app, choose "eKYC with FaceAuth", enter the reference number and OTP, and look at the camera in good light.
  5. You receive a 14-digit OTR number by SMS. Write it down: you need it for every scholarship, every year.
  • If face authentication keeps failing, check in the mAadhaar app that your biometrics are not locked. A locked biometric makes it fail.
  • The OTR is not an application. You still have to apply for each scheme.
