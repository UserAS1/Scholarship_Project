How to apply on the Odisha State Scholarship Portal

Steps read on the official portal

For: Post-matric scholarships for SC, ST, OBC and SEBC students, and state schemes such as e-Medhabruti, Gopabandhu, Kalinga Sikhya Sathi, Banishree, and the Junior Merit and Pathani Samanta scholarships for Class 11 and 12.

Dates: On the portal (checked 14 November 2026): Junior Merit and Pathani Samanta Mathematics Talent (Class 11–12, 2026-27) extended to 25 November 2026; Kalinga Sikhya Sathi (fresh) and Banishree 2026-27 open. Other dates are posted department by department under Announcements.

Keep ready
  • Aadhaar with a working linked mobile number (registration is by Aadhaar OTP)
  • Caste and income certificates
  • Last marksheet, admission letter and bonafide certificate
  • Bank account details
Steps
  1. On the portal, use Check Eligibility to see which schemes you may get.
  2. Register once with your Aadhaar number and the OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile. Your user ID and password come by SMS and email.
  3. Log in, choose the department and scheme, and fill in your student, academic, eligibility and bank details. You can save and come back as often as you like.
  4. Submit, then download and print the form. After you submit you cannot edit it.

After you submit: Your institute verifies it, then the district welfare officer. If you spot a mistake after submitting, ask your institute's scholarship person to "revert" it so you can correct it. If your institute or course is not in the list, the institute must ask the district welfare officer to add it. Track it under Know Your Status.

Help: Sanjog helpline 155335 or toll-free 1800-345-6770, 9 am to 5 pm on government working days.

Checked 14 November 2026. Your name, gender and date of birth are taken from Aadhaar and cannot be changed in the form, so correct them in Aadhaar first. Portals change their screens often; if a step looks different, follow the portal and tell us.
