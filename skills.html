Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY 4.0)

Who can applyIndian citizens aged 15 to 45 (the age range depends on the course), with Aadhaar. Each course has its own education requirement.
What you getFree short-term courses (a few weeks to a few months) in hundreds of trades, including new ones such as AI, drones and EV servicing, with a national certificate. Some centres offer placement help.
How to applyRegister on the Skill India Digital Hub with your Aadhaar-linked mobile number, pick a course and a centre near you.

Age range and the free training are from a NIELIT notice (a government body). Listings mention a completion bonus at some centres; we have not confirmed it. Document we read.

Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (DDU-GKY)

Who can applyRural youth aged 15 to 35 from poor households (older for women, SC/ST and disabled candidates, as listed).
What you getFree training of 3 to 12 months, often residential with free food and stay, followed by a job placement. A new phase started batches from October 2026.
How to applyContact your State Rural Livelihoods Mission or a DDU-GKY training centre in your district.

Eligibility and benefits from the West Bengal government's scheme page; the October 2026 batches from news reports. Document we read.

Rural Self-Employment Training Institutes (RSETIs)

Who can applyRural unemployed youth who want to start their own small business.
What you getFree residential training in a trade or business, with help afterwards to start up and get a bank loan.
How to applyFind the RSETI in your district (usually run by the lead bank) and ask about the next batch.

From news reports of the ministry's announcements.

Jan Shikshan Sansthan (JSS)

Who can applyPeople aged 15 to 45 who cannot read and write, have studied up to Class 8, or left school before Class 12. Women, SC, ST, OBC and minorities get priority.
What you getFree local vocational courses run by NGOs near your home, with nationally recognised courses.
How to applyFind your district's Jan Shikshan Sansthan on the JSS portal and register for a course.

Who it is for is from a government press release. Document we read.

PM Vishwakarma

Who can applyArtisans and craftspeople in 18 traditional family trades (carpenter, blacksmith, potter, tailor, cobbler and others). One member per family.
What you getBasic training of 5–7 days and advanced training of 15 days or more, paid at ₹500 a day; a toolkit grant of ₹15,000; and loans up to ₹3 lakh without collateral at 5% interest.
How to applyRegister at a Common Service Centre (CSC) with Aadhaar; verification follows at the village or town level.

Amounts read on the government scheme page. Document we read.

Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs)

Who can applyUsually Class 8 or Class 10 pass, depending on the trade. Admission is through your state's ITI admission portal.
What you getOne- and two-year trade courses with a national certificate (NCVT). Government ITIs charge low fees, and the ITI scholarships in your results can apply.
How to applyApply on your state's ITI admission portal. Use our college finder to check an ITI and its grade.

ITI details come from the national list of ITIs in our college finder.
