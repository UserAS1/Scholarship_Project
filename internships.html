Internships and apprenticeships across India

Government internships and apprenticeship schemes open to students from any state. Many pay a stipend; some are unpaid but count on a CV. Each card says how we checked it. Checked 14 Nov 2026.

National portals and schemes

Ministry of Corporate AffairsFrom news reports and listings

PM Internship Scheme

Who can applyAged 21 to 24, not in a full-time job or full-time course (distance and online learners can apply). Class 10 pass or above, including ITI, diploma and degree holders. Not if anyone in your family earns over ₹8 lakh a year or holds a government job. Graduates of some top institutes and holders of professional qualifications such as CA, MBBS or MBA are left out: check the FAQ.
What you get₹5,000 a month for 12 months (₹4,500 from the government, ₹500 from the company), plus ₹6,000 once on joining. Insurance cover is included.
WhenOpens in rounds. Check the portal for the current round.

Amounts and rules from news reports and listings, which agree with each other. The portal could not be read. Some 2026 reports mention higher amounts and a wider age range; go by the portal.

National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS)

Who can applyGraduates and diploma holders in any stream, usually within three years of finishing, and final-year students in apprenticeship-embedded courses. Training lasts 6 to 12 months in a company or public-sector body.
What you getAt least ₹12,300 a month for graduates and ₹10,900 for diploma holders from 2026-27. The government pays half of the minimum straight to you; the employer pays the rest, and many pay more.
WhenAll year. Register once, then apply to openings listed on the portal.

Rates from a Board of Apprenticeship Training presentation (June 2026) published by a central university. Document we read.

All India Council for Technical EducationFrom news reports and listings

AICTE National Internship Portal

Who can applyStudents of any year, mainly in engineering, management, diploma and other technical courses. Free to register.
What you getSome listings are paid and some are not; it depends on the company. Registration and applying are free.
WhenAll year.

Only internship.aicte-india.org is the official portal. Any site that charges a fee in AICTE's name is fake. Internships also count for credits in AICTE-approved courses.

NITI Aayog, New DelhiFrom news reports and listings

NITI Aayog Internship Scheme

Who can applyUndergraduate and postgraduate students and research scholars at a recognised university, with good marks (listings mention 70% for postgraduates). Recent graduates may apply within six months of their result.
What you getUnpaid. A certificate is given if you complete the period.
WhenApply from the 1st to the 10th of each month, for internships starting later. Six weeks to six months, on site in New Delhi.

Ministry of External Affairs Internship

Who can applyIndian graduates and final-year students, up to 25 years old (as reported).
What you get₹10,000 a month, plus help with one return air fare for students from far away. You arrange your own stay in Delhi.
WhenTwo terms a year; up to 30 interns each. The October 2026 intake closed on 27 September 2026.

DRDO lab internships

Who can applyMostly final-year engineering and science students with good marks (often 75% or CGPA 7.5), from AICTE- or UGC-approved institutions.
What you getMany labs pay ₹5,000 a month for six months (₹30,000 in total), in instalments. Some are unpaid.
WhenEach lab advertises separately, often in spring for internships from July. Several apply by post.
