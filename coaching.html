Free Coaching for SC and OBC students

Who can applySC and OBC students with family income up to ₹8 lakh; children under the PM CARES scheme with no income or caste rule.
What you getCoaching fees paid to an empanelled institute, plus a monthly stipend while you study. Covers civil services, state PSC, SSC, railway and bank exams, and entrance exams such as JEE, NEET and CLAT.
How to applyApplications are invited online each year; the scheme's FAQ is on the National Scholarship Portal.

Mukhyamantri Anuprati Coaching Yojana

Who can applyRajasthan students from SC, ST, OBC, MBC, EWS and minority communities, family income up to ₹8 lakh. 30,000 seats a year.
What you getFree coaching at empanelled institutes for competitive and entrance exams, with help for hostel and food if you move to a coaching city.

Jai Bhim Mukhyamantri Pratibha Vikas Yojana

Who can applyDelhi students from SC, ST, OBC and EWS families with income up to ₹8 lakh, who passed Class 10 and 12 in Delhi.
What you getCoaching fees paid (up to ₹1 lakh for civil services, up to ₹50,000 for engineering, medical, law and management entrance) and ₹2,500 a month while you study.
